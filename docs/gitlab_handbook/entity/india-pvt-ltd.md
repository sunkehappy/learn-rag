### Provident Fund and National Pension Scheme

#### Provident Fund

GitLab contributes to team member’s Provident Funds, which is outlined in your employment agreement. It ensures retirement benefits and family pensions on death in service.

- Member Contribution(% of base salary):
  - EPF: 3.67% and EPS: 8.33%, Total Contribution: 12%
- Employer Contribution(% of base salary):
  - EPF: 12%, EPS: None

Team members will declare their desired provident fund contributions (up to salary ceiling or actual compensation) during onboarding.

<details markdown="1">

<summary>Reducing PF Contributions</summary>

Team members select their Provident Fund election when they join GitLab. If you initially elected to cap your PF contribution, you cannot request to increase it later. If you elected 12% PF contributions, you may request to reduce to the capped amount during the April enrollment period, however please note: this will reduce both employer matching contributions and your overall CTC. Your 'fixed compensation' component will remain unchanged and will not be adjusted to offset the reduction.

To request a reduction, submit a formal written request via [HelpLab](https://helplab.gitlab.systems/esc) acknowledging the impact to your CTC and employer contributions prior to April 6th to reflect the change in the next Tax year.

</details>

<details markdown="1">

<summary>Voluntary Provident Fund Elections</summary>

Team members can opt to make additional voluntary provident fund contributions at the start of employment, with the ability to update their elections each April. Payroll will communicate with team members during this annual election period.

</details>

<details markdown="1">

<summary>Provident fund (PF) process</summary>

1. New team members will advise on their provident fund election at the employment agreement stage (12% or capped)
1. Payroll will collect team members UAN (Universal Account Number) during onboarding.
1. If this is the team members’ first provident fund, and they do not have a UAN, payroll can generate one, after the team member completes their Workday and Papaya onboarding tasks.
1. Monthly contributions are automatically deducted from salary.
1. Annual PF statements can be found on the [EPFO website](https://unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in/) using your UAN and password.
1. PF Withdrawals require proper documentation. For more details please refer to the [EPFO portal](https://www.epfindia.gov.in/site_en/WhichClaimForm.php).

Note, team members may be eligible to opt out of the Provident fund if they meet all these conditions:

- Basic salary exceeds 15,000 INR per month
- Have never contributed to EPF before
- Submits Form 11 at the time of joining. This decision must be made upfront during onboarding

</details>

#### National Pension Scheme

GitLab India offers the National Pension Scheme (NPS), a voluntary government-sponsored retirement savings program regulated by the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA). NPS allows you to contribute a portion of your salary toward long-term retirement savings, which are invested and grow over time until withdrawal at retirement age. For a full overview of how the plan works, eligibility, and what happens to your contributions, please review the [NPS FAQ](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1xoUo5NOZU8-k9kpcn7AEa_kXC2jjiU7kieQWxXXB3Hw/edit?tab=t.0) 

<details markdown="1">

<summary>Contributions</summary>

NPS is voluntary. You may elect to contribute between 1% and 14% of your basic salary. GitLab does not make an employer contribution to NPS — your elected amount will be deducted from your salary and remitted to your individual NPS account each month. 

</details>

<details markdown="1">

<summary>Enrollment</summary>

New hires are asked to share their NPS election details (contribution percentage and PRAN number) during the offer process via a reference check questionnaire in Greenhouse. If you do not wish to participate select 0%. 

Existing team members can enroll or update their contribution during the bi-annual election windows in April and November. To submit an election, open a HelpLab People Ops ticket with your PRAN number and desired contribution percentage. Outside of these windows, contribution percentages cannot be changed.

</details>

<details markdown="1">

<summary>Your PRAN (Permanent Retirement Account Number)</summary>

Your PRAN is the unique identifier that routes contributions to your NPS account.

Already have a PRAN? You're all set — provide it during the enrollment process.
Don't have a PRAN? Register for a [Corporate NPS account through KFintech CRA](https://cra.kfintech.com/poponline/popverification?AuthID=37BD5C5346F9171C7A1DBEE97E20F90DB0B1061F064A8B8A0C27C37CE272DD8E0DFF1C72A0590DA64BF1D2A953A7DFBB). See the [step-by-step enrollment instructions](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1tg9pegN3GLWqdTDHXscfpWgraU21TP0I/view?usp=sharing) for guidance.

⚠️ Important: When registering for a new PRAN, select "Corporate Subscriber" as your Applicant Type. This ensures your PRAN is mapped to GitLab's corporate NPS account. Selecting the wrong type will delay your enrollment.

</details>

### India Specific Leave

Below is the information related to India’s statutory leave policies. Team members may also be eligible for GitLab company leave policies as well, which will run concurrently with statutory policies whenever allowed by law.

_Team members must enter all scheduled time off at least 30 days prior to the start date to prevent delays or discrepancies in their pay. Any unplanned time away must be entered as soon as possible._

#### Statutory Leave Entitlement

**Annual Leave**: Team members are entitled to 18 days of Earned Leave per year. Team members accrue 1.5 days (or 12 hours) per month.

- Workers (indiviual contributors) can accrue up to a maximum of 45 days. However, workers can only carry over 30 days per year. Any days in excess of that will need to be encashed at the end of each calendar year.
- People managers can accrue up to a maximum for 18 days, and are ineligible to carry over or encash any days in excess of that.

</details>

<details markdown="1">

<summary>India Sick and Casual Leave</summary>

- **India Sick Leave**: 12 days annually. The time can be taken intermittently or continuously and will be applied concurrently with [GitLab's Sick Time](/handbook/people-group/time-off-and-absence/time-off-types/#sick-time) policy.
  - No carry forward or encashment allowed. Balance expires at the end of the calendar year.
- **India Casual Leave**: 3 days annually. The time can be taken intermittently or continuously.
  - No carry forward or encashment allowed. Balance expires at the end of the calendar year.

**Note:** One exception: if a team member is actively on sick/casual leave and their employment ends, they will be entitled to be paid the remaining balance of that leave.

You can request either of these leaves by selecting it from the Leaves or Intermittent Leaves drop-down in Workday. The request will route to the Absence Management team for review, and we will follow-up with you with more details.

</details>
