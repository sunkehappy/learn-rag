As a GitLab contributor, you're part of a community that helps build one of the world's largest open source projects.
We want to make it easy for you to share and celebrate your contributions!

## Ways to showcase your contributions

### Contributor Stats Banner

Add a dynamic stats banner to your [GitLab profile README](https://docs.gitlab.com/user/profile/#add-details-to-your-profile-with-a-readme), personal website, or anywhere you can embed an SVG.
The banner displays your contribution statistics and updates automatically each time the banner is loaded.

[Learn how to add the Contributor Stats Banner →](/handbook/marketing/developer-relations/engineering/showcase-your-contributions/stats-banner)

### LinkedIn

Your contributions to GitLab are a valuable part of your professional portfolio.
Please see our guidance for highlighting your open source work on LinkedIn in a way that best represents your experience.

[Learn how to add your contributions to LinkedIn →](/handbook/marketing/developer-relations/engineering/showcase-your-contributions/linkedin)
