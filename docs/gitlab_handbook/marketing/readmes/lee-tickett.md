## About me

I'm Lee Tickett, a Fullstack Engineer in the [Developer Relations Engineering team](/handbook/marketing/developer-relations/engineering/).
I joined GitLab in October 2022 after being a customer, contributor, and [core team member](https://about.gitlab.com/community/core-team/).

I live near London in Southend-on-Sea, UK.
I typically work morning through mid-afternoon, break for family time, then work a little later in the evening.

## How I work

- I move fast.
- I love to build POCs, fail fast, and iterate.
- I take a [bias for action](/handbook/values/#operate-with-a-bias-for-action),
  [have short toes](/handbook/values/#short-toes),
  and a [low level of shame](/handbook/values/#low-level-of-shame-when-dogfooding) when pursuing results.
- I love working in public, collaborating, bouncing ideas around, and being transparent.
- I work from my GitLab TODO queue, which I aim to keep around one day deep.

**What helps me:**

- Clear issues and merge requests.
- Documented processes and the ability to self-serve.
- Opportunities to learn and unblock myself.
- Data and empathy when disagreeing.

**What slows me down:**

- Red tape
- Inability to iterate
- Dependencies on others when I can't self-serve
