## My Communication Preferences

My manager, Manu Narayan, said it best, so I'm unashamedly borrowing his framework:

1. **Slack:** My go-to for day-to-day communication. I'm usually active throughout the day and often check in after hours. If you get a late-night message from me, no need to respond until the next business day unless I say otherwise.

1. **Documents:** I'm a big believer that complex or strategic topics deserve a proper document. It's how I think, review, and give feedback. Tag me in comments with specific questions and I'll get back to you.

1. **Meetings:** I have a high bar for meetings. If we can solve it asynchronously, let's do that. If we truly need to get together, please come with a clear agenda and any relevant pre-reads so we make the most of our time together.

1. **Text/Phone:** This is my 911. Please reserve it for true emergencies or anything genuinely urgent that can't wait.
