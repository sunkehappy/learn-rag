## SA Engagement Playbooks

SA Engagement Playbooks are end-to-end guides for specific selling or engagement motions. A playbook differs from a process (which defines mandatory steps with system touchpoints) and a practice (which describes skills an SA is encouraged to develop). A playbook provides a structured, repeatable guide for a particular customer engagement or sales motion from start to finish.
