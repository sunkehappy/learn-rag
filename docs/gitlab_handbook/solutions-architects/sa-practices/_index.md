## SA Practices

SA Practices are skills and behaviors that Solutions Architects are encouraged to develop, with judgment and discretion. They differ from [SA Processes](/handbook/solutions-architects/processes/) (mandatory steps with defined system touchpoints) and [SA Engagement Playbooks](/handbook/solutions-architects/playbooks/) (end-to-end guides for specific selling or engagement motions).
