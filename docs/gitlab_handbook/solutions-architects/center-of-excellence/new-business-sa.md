## Who We Are

The New Business Solutions Architecture (SA) team is a group of pre-sales technical advisors focused on helping GitLab prospects understand and evaluate the GitLab platform. We work closely with Account Executives across EMEA and AMER to support First-Order opportunities in the Mid-Market and Enterprise segments.

We operate as part of the [Solutions Architecture Center of Excellence](/handbook/solutions-architects/center-of-excellence/). This means our work goes beyond individual customer engagements: every interaction informs the playbooks, approaches, and reusable assets we build to systematically improve how GitLab acquires new business at scale.

Our mission is to guide prospects through a focused, outcome-driven technical evaluation while continuously distilling field experience into better ways of engaging future customers.

## What We Do

We engage at the intersection of technical depth and business value. When a prospect is ready to evaluate GitLab, we help by:

- Running discovery to understand current toolchains, workflows, and goals
- Delivering tailored demos and technical presentations aligned to customer use cases
- Designing and facilitating technical evaluations and workshops
- Addressing technical questions and objections throughout the sales cycle
- Creating reusable assets and enablement content that scale our team's reach

Equally important, we treat our customer engagements as a source of organisational learning. Patterns we observe in the field feed directly into playbooks, templates, and approaches that raise the quality and consistency of new business acquisition across the team.

## How We Operate

We align tightly with our Account Executive partners and engage once an opportunity has been qualified and is ready for technical evaluation. Our engagements follow a consistent, phased approach:

1. **Research**: Understand the customer's motivation, current state, and success criteria
2. **Plan**: Co-create a focused technical validation plan with the customer and AE
3. **Implement**: Execute the plan, validate use cases, and support the path to a confident purchase decision

We prioritise opportunities where there is a clear business need, an identified champion, and a defined evaluation timeline. Where SA involvement isn't warranted, we help equip the Sales Team to handle technical conversations independently.

## How to Engage Us

### For Sales Team Members

SA requests are managed directly in Salesforce. To request SA support:

1. Navigate to the relevant Opportunity in SFDC
2. Click **Request Support → SA Request**
3. Fill in the SA ask, language preference, perceived net ARR, due date, why now, and key pain points

Your request automatically creates a Case routed to the SA team. You'll continue to receive notifications in Slack, and an SA will follow up to align on next steps.
